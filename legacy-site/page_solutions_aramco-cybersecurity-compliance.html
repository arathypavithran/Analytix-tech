Why it matters?

Aramco’s SACS-002 standard requires all third-party vendors, contractors, and suppliers to meet strict cybersecurity controls before entering contracts with Saudi Aramco. This is not a formality. It involves a formal audit by accredited assessors, directly affecting your contract status. Non-compliance can lead to suspension, causing lost revenue, project delays, and strained client relationships. The challenge is that many vendors underestimate the preparation required. SACS-002 covers 12 security domains, including technical controls and documented proof such as policies, access management, and incident response. Starting early is critical, as many vendors only recognise gaps after failing their first audit.

How it Works

We follow a structured four-phase process to help you achieve full audit readiness from your current position. There’s no confusion and no wasted time.

[Process]

Phase 1

Gap Assessment (Days 1–5)

We assess your existing IT infrastructure, policies, and security practices against all 12 SACS-002 domains. By the end of this phase, you will get a detailed gap report that outlines what is present, what is missing, and what requires updates.

Phase 2

Remediation Plan (Days 6–10)

Based on the gap report, we create a prioritized action plan. You will know which gaps are crucial for the audit, which can be tackled simultaneously, and who is responsible for each task. Everything aligns with the SACS-002 control requirements.

Phase 3

Implementation (Days 11–25)

Our team works with your team to address the identified gaps. This involves setting up technical controls, drafting and finalizing necessary policies, implementing monitoring tools, and conducting staff awareness sessions. If third-party tools are required, we assist you in choosing and setting them up.

Phase 4

Audit Preparation and Documentation (Days 26–30)

We compile all necessary evidence, perform an internal pre-audit review, and get your team ready for the assessment. When the assessor walks in, you should be prepared for everything.

Does this apply to you?

SACS-002 compliance is mandatory for any organization that operates as a third-party vendor, supplier, or contractor for Saudi Aramco, no matter the size of the company or the nature of the work. This includes various types of businesses:

Construction contractors engaged in Aramco facilities or infrastructure projects

Manufacturing suppliers delivering equipment, components, or materials for Aramco operations

IT service providers that assist Aramco with its systems, networks, or technology infrastructure

Engineering and consulting firms that deliver technical or advisory services

Logistics and transport companies managing Aramco's supply chain or materials transportation

Professional services providers in finance, legal, HR, or other support areas

If your organization is working with Saudi Aramco, or trying to secure a contract, the SACS-002 audit is a key step. Addressing compliance early minimizes disruption to your existing operations and timelines.
